## Showmi | Live-Shopping Marketplace

Showmi is a mobile-first, video-led shopping marketplace designed to help South African small businesses, informal traders, creators and entrepreneurs reach customers through shoppable content and live shopping.

Buyers can discover products through the LIVE feed or Showroom, add items to their cart and place orders. Sellers can manage their shops, view incoming orders, update dispatch status and access a demonstration wallet.

### Live Demo

**Try Showmi:** https://showmi-live-shopping-rho.vercel.app/

### How to Test the Demo

**1. Buyer journey**
- Sign in using the Buyer Demo account.
- Browse the LIVE feed or Showroom.
- Select a product, add it to the cart and proceed through checkout.
- Ensure you buy from The Cupcake Corner store as the seller demo is linked to this store.
- View the order and test the available order-status actions.
- Once the seller has clicked on dispatch, you can click on Item Received. 

**2. Seller journey**
- Sign in using the Seller Demo account for The Cupcake Corner.
- Open Seller Studio and locate the incoming order.
- Mark the seller's item as dispatched.
- Explore the seller wallet and simulated withdrawal request.

**3. Buyer and seller interaction**
- The buyer places an order.
- The seller marks the item as dispatched.
- The buyer confirms receipt by selecting **Item Received**.
- In the intended workflow, buyer confirmation triggers the release of the payment allocated to the seller.

### Demonstration Notice

Payments and withdrawals are simulated. The demo does not process real payments or transfer real funds. Some transaction, wallet and order-management behaviour requires further development before a production launch.

### Development Priorities

- Secure, persistent storage and reliable transaction processing.
- Accurate order-level and seller-level payment calculations.
- Seller verification, fraud prevention and buyer-protection processes.
- Improved mobile responsiveness and performance on limited data connections.

### Project Vision

Showmi aims to make online shopping more interactive by bringing product discovery, live demonstrations and the buying journey together, while creating more opportunities for small businesses to reach customers.
